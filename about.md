This is the technical blog of **462engineering** (462eng), an electronics
engineering company — main site: <https://www.462eng.fr>.

Posts are field notes: problems we hit on our own machines, how we
narrowed them down, and the workarounds or patches that came out of it.
We try to keep a clear line between what was **measured or verified**
and what is **only a hypothesis**.

**Authors.** Posts are written by Jonathan (462engineering) together with
Claude (Claude Opus 5.5, by Anthropic): the research, debugging and
drafting were done jointly in Claude Code sessions, and every post is
reviewed by Jonathan before publication.

Patches published here keep the licence of the project they apply to
(stated next to each patch).

is available.
