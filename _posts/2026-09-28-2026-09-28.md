A summary of the day. Each problem below was found while working on the
previous one, so this post follows them in order. Details are in the
linked posts.

## Bring-up: Debian 13, RDNA4, Valve Index

The GPU box is a Debian 13 machine with a Radeon AI PRO R9700 (RDNA4).
RDNA4 needs the kernel, Mesa and firmware from trixie-backports. evdi
needs version 1.15.1 to build on kernel 7.1. We also hit a few
boot-time problems (a missing display manager unit, an initramfs that
picked up `.link` backups) and a few Steam and Index details.
→ [Small notes: Debian 13 + RDNA4 + Index]({% post_url 2026-09-28-notes-debian-13-rdna4-index %})

## Playing from the desk: sRemFB

To use the GPU box from the workstation, sRemFB creates an evdi virtual
display on it and streams uncompressed or LZ4 damage rectangles to a new
windowed viewer. Keyboard, mouse and gamepad go back over uinput, and
audio over UDP. Key to pixel: 8.7 ms median on 2.5 GbE.
→ [Playing on the GPU box from the desk, without a video codec]({% post_url 2026-09-28-sremfb-playing-on-the-gpu-box-without-a-codec %})

## SteamVR judder, and moving to Monado

In SteamVR, menus and overlays judder. On Linux, SteamVR runs without
async reprojection. Enabling it, or asking for 120 Hz, ends with
`flip_done timed out` and a killed compositor. Monado with SteamVR's own
Lighthouse driver, xrizer and WayVR runs smoothly at 90, 120 and 144 Hz
on the same hardware.
→ [SteamVR on Linux: overlay/UI judder on RDNA4, and why Monado fixes it]({% post_url 2026-09-28-steamvr-linux-overlay-judder-rdna4-monado %})

## mutter never forgets an unplugged GPU

mutter never removes a GPU that has been unplugged, and Xwayland keeps
its DRM fd. Every removed evdi card leaves a stale DRM lease device. In
the end, SteamVR and Monado both stall on them.
→ [mutter never forgets an unplugged GPU (and what it does to VR)]({% post_url 2026-09-28-mutter-never-forgets-unplugged-gpu %})

## How the problems connect

1. **sRemFB** creates **evdi cards** on the GPU box.
2. With evdi cards present, **SteamVR's DRM lease breaks**
   (`dc_stream_state is NULL`, `flip_done timed out`).
3. The first fix was to **remove the evdi cards** before starting VR.
4. But **mutter never forgets a removed GPU**. Each removed card leaves a
   stale `wp_drm_lease_device_v1` global, and Xwayland keeps its fd.
5. The **stale globals block VR**: SteamVR loops after
   `LeaseDevice_Done`, and Monado waits forever for `done` events, or
   picks a stale device that advertises the Index's connector.
6. With a **patched Monado** (bounded wait, no silent fallback, optional
   DRM node pinning), Monado ignores the stale devices. It also runs
   fine **alongside evdi**, which SteamVR does not.
7. So there is no longer any reason to remove evdi cards:
   **`sremfb-server` now runs permanently**. No cards are removed, so no
   stale devices are created.

A mutter patch for the root cause exists but **has not been tested**. It
is published as an RFC in the mutter post.
