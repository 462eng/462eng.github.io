## The LXI bench and what it can do

A power supply, an electronic load and an oscilloscope on the network,
driven as one by our tool `462lxi`. What they can source, absorb and
observe, and what they do together. A certified multimeter then showed
that the supply was off, not the load, and that an idle supply channel
quietly sinks 40 mA. Two of its channels in series now make one 60 V
supply, checked on the bench up to 50 V.
→ [The LXI bench and what it can do]({% post_url 2026-10-31-lxi-bench-three-instruments-one-command-line %})

## An 18650 measured, modelled and replayed

An old Samsung 25R charged to within a millivolt, discharged in 1 A
pulses, and turned into a model with two polarisation branches that
replays the real discharge to 3.5 mV RMS. The power supply can then
behave like that cell, new or aged, and with the load, take a charge
as well as give one. Three more cells from the same batch followed:
a matched pair worth keeping, and a worn pair.
