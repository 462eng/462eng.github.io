## HAWK: tomorrow's EDA, yesterday's best idea

HAWK is the schematic and PCB editor we have been writing since July.
It brings back the live link between schematic and board that Proteus
had in the 1990s, as a single document. It now has an assisted design
mode. Claude reads compact views and sends transactional batches of
operations, while HAWK places, routes, checks and simulates locally.
Its first job was a review of a real board from 2020, and it found
every design error.
→ [HAWK: tomorrow's EDA, yesterday's best idea]({% post_url 2026-10-23-hawk-pcb-design-claude-decides-hawk-computes %})

## TuyaBeGone 1.0

The free firmware for the LSC camera met a new camera whose factory
U-Boot erases only the first 4 KiB of each flash area. The SD card
install now has U-Boot write only a relay of under 4 KiB, which finds
U-Boot's own commands in memory and starts the real installer from the
card. Validated on both U-Boot versions, this is version 1.0.
→ [TuyaBeGone 1.0: a relay for a lazy U-Boot]({% post_url 2026-10-23-tuyabegone-1-0-u-boot-relay %})

## sRemFB: a KVM mode

A new backend turns a Raspberry Pi 4 with an HDMI-to-CSI bridge into a
KVM over IP for the existing sRemFB viewer. It handles HDMI capture with
damage computed by tiles, keyboard and mouse through a USB HID gadget,
and the Pi's hardware H.264 encoder as a fallback. The viewer can now
decode H.264. It is tested on a PC, and the Pi comes next.
→ [sRemFB KVM: HDMI in, USB HID out, on a Pi 4]({% post_url 2026-10-23-sremfb-kvm-hdmi-capture-raspberry-pi %})
