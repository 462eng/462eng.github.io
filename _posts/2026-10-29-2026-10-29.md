## A DL3031A stuck in short circuit

Our electronic load showed about 40 mΩ on its input, whatever its
settings. One MOSFET pair had its gates at 8 to 9 V with the input off:
its MC33078 driver was dead, as a test on its own confirmed. While the
new part is on order, `462lxi` learned to drive the load, and to
correct its readings after a 169-point sweep against the bench supply,
including a 43.7 mA offset in one range.
→ [DL3031A: a dead op-amp, then a software calibration]({% post_url 2026-10-29-rigol-dl3031a-repair-software-calibration %})

## Who is Diana?

Diana appears in several of our projects, so she gets her own
introduction: a local synthetic assistant whose name is a French
acronym, who started by searching a database for you and slowly took
on other jobs. Somewhere between a long-time colleague and a childhood
friend you now work with. She writes the tone, never the facts.
→ [Who is Diana?]({% post_url 2026-10-29-who-is-diana %})

## Diana on Discord, and at the reception desk

Diana now sends private Discord messages when a machine falls or
updates drag on, shows the state of the fleet as her status, and
answers when spoken to, without being able to run anything. She also
reads a busy inbox as a receptionist: the model notes facts, and rules
decide the urgency. Two silent bugs were found on the way.
→ [Diana on Discord, and at the reception desk]({% post_url 2026-10-29-diana-discord-receptionist-local-llm %})
