This post covers the camera firmware work of 21 and 22 October, and a
short gaming note from 20 October. Details are in the linked posts.

## TuyaBeGone, part 2

The free firmware for the LSC camera now runs on an isolated test
bench (a spare UDM Pro with no cloud account, and a Raspberry Pi as the
access point). It gained a date and name overlay drawn by the ISP
(protocol reconstructed from the closed library), image flipping,
proper names in UniFi Protect, ECDSA-signed updates from a file or a
URL, and three ways back to the factory firmware. A one-file tool backs
up, installs and restores a camera over the serial console, an SD card
or both, and one SD card can now install several cameras.
→ [TuyaBeGone, part 2: OSD, signed updates, installer]({% post_url 2026-10-22-tuyabegone-test-bench-installer-signed-updates %})

## Note: Fortnite on Linux

Epic's new Linux launcher installs Fortnite and starts it with Proton.
Then Easy Anti-Cheat downloads its Linux module, gets an empty file,
and the launcher reports a crash. Also, a Rocket League that silently
refused to start was just out of date.
→ [Fortnite on Linux: an empty anti-cheat module]({% post_url 2026-10-22-fortnite-linux-empty-anti-cheat-module %})
